Our story

Creole BBQ meets the Big Easy

Welcome to Davis Northwest, where the vibrant flavors of New Orleans meet smoky, Southern-style barbecue. Our chefs blend traditional Creole spices with time-honored BBQ techniques, creating a fusion that delights your taste buds with every bite.

Expect slow-cooked ribs, tender smoked meats and succulent chicken, all infused with that New Orleans zest. Whether you're craving bold Creole flavor or comforting, smoky BBQ, our menu offers the best of both worlds.

“From our hearts, to your table.”Willie Davis III, owner of Davis N.W.
